Oracle Database Free 26ai / PS_ASSIGNMENT / FREEPDB1
Query executed:
SELECT d.NAME AS DEPARTMENT_NAME, SUM(e.SALARY) AS TOTAL_MONTHLY_SALARY
FROM MyEmployee e JOIN MyDepartment d ON e.DEPT_ID = d.DEPT_ID
WHERE UPPER(d.NAME) <> 'SALES'
GROUP BY d.NAME
HAVING SUM(e.SALARY) > 2500;
